# Pre-State Coaches' Poll: Three New No. 1s Head to Fortress Obetz

The state meet is Saturday, Nov. 7, at Fortress Obetz, and the Ohio Association of Track and Cross Country Coaches (OATCCC) Pre-State coaches' poll is out. Three divisions have a new No. 1: Lexington in Division II boys, Upper Arlington in Division I girls and Liberty Center in Division IV girls. Woodridge in Division III boys is the only unanimous pick, with all 12 first-place votes.

The closest race at the top is Division IV boys, where Mount Gilead leads Russia by three points, 283 to 280.

## Boys

**Division I.** Springboro stays No. 1 with 238 points and 10 of the 12 first-place votes. Mason took the other two and moved from third to second at 226. Mentor is third at 214, Beavercreek fourth at 199 and St Xavier fifth at 185. Little Miami climbed seven spots to No. 10, and Kings six to No. 18. This week's Division I poll ranks 20 teams, down from 24.

**Division II.** Lexington jumped from No. 7 to No. 1 with 275 points and four first-place votes. Bowling Green is four points back at 271 with three, Big Walnut is third at 255 with one, and Kenston is fourth at 246 with two. Toledo St Francis, second in the Pre-Regional poll, is fifth at 232. Massillon Perry made the biggest climb, from No. 20 to No. 9, and Hoover fell from No. 4 to No. 14. Sylvania Northview, Carroll and Bellefontaine join the poll at Nos. 22 through 24.

**Division III.** Woodridge is unanimous again at 288 points. Versailles is second at 271, followed by Fairless (263), West Liberty-Salem (251) and Bryan (231). Tuscarawas Valley made the biggest move, from No. 24 to No. 15, and Gilmour Academy enters the poll at No. 16.

**Division IV.** Mount Gilead holds No. 1 with 283 points and seven first-place votes. Russia has the other five and trails by three at 280. Tinora is third at 258, Convoy Crestview fourth at 249 and Legacy Christian fifth at 238. Shenandoah climbed nine spots to No. 14.

## Girls

**Division I.** Upper Arlington and Milford traded places. Upper Arlington is No. 1 with 238 points and 10 first-place votes, and Milford is second at 230 with the other two. Springboro is third at 202, Gahanna Lincoln fourth at 193 and Hilliard Davidson fifth at 189. Olentangy Berlin (17 to 12) and Westerville South (21 to 16) each climbed five spots. Like the boys, the Division I girls poll ranks 20 teams this week.

**Division II.** Watterson is unchanged at the top with 286 points and 11 of the 12 first-place votes. Aurora is second at 276 with the other one. Big Walnut (246), Fremont Ross (240) and Granville (239) round out the top five. Lexington made the biggest jump, from No. 22 to No. 10, and Cleveland St Joseph Academy and Westlake each climbed eight spots. Wadsworth slid from No. 10 to No. 19.

**Division III.** Huron has 287 points and 11 first-place votes. Oakwood is second at 276 with the other one, then Minerva (260), Ottawa Glandorf (245) and Marlington (241). Tuscarawas Valley climbed 10 spots to No. 8, and Hathaway Brown fell from No. 13 to No. 22.

**Division IV.** Liberty Center moved from fourth to first with 280 points and nine first-place votes. Grandview Heights is second at 272 with two, and Ottawa Hills, No. 1 in the Pre-Regional poll, is third at 269 with one. Minster is fourth at 255 and Rittman fifth at 241. Milan Edison climbed eight spots to No. 14.
