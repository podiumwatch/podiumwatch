# The opening view

Girls Division III begins with two teams under 140 points and four under 190. Huron holds number one at 114. Oakwood follows at 134. Minerva is third at 175, and Woodridge is fourth at 186. The opening order gives Huron a real advantage, but it also shows multiple ways for the race to tighten.

Huron's first five are efficient and connected. Oakwood answers with four scorers inside the first 26 before its fifth lands at 63. Minerva places two inside the first eight, then pays more at the fifth position. Woodridge starts with two scorers in the first ten and puts its fifth and sixth side by side. Ottawa-Glandorf is fifth with one of the most compact groups in the division.

This is a classification where the fifth runner is not a talking point added at the end. It is the central storyline. Several contenders already have the front end to compete. The teams that turn their last scoring place into a smaller number can change the championship picture quickly.

# How the rankings were created

These rankings use returning runners and recorded 2025 5K performances. Teams were placed in their official 2026 OHSAA divisions. The twenty five fastest eligible teams by five runner average formed each simulated field, with up to seven returners entered per team.

Five runners scored. Sixth and seventh runners displaced opponents and decided ties. The lowest score won, with ties settled by the sixth runner and then the seventh. The number beside a team is its simulated cross country score, not its total time or five runner average.

The recorded performances came from different courses, meets, weather conditions, and points in the 2025 season. No course adjustment was applied. The result measures returning strength through one consistent scoring model, but it does not guarantee what will happen when teams race in 2026.
