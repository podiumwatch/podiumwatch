Regionals are done. The State field is set: 184 teams and 368 individuals across eight races, all of them earned on Saturday at Troy, Pickerington, Boardman and Tiffin.

Podium Watch now takes exactly that field and scores it. The [Mock State hub](/mock-postseason/real/?tab=state) puts every qualifier in one race per division and runs the meet on the time each runner posted at regionals on Oct. 31. No season bests, no guesses about who might qualify. The real State lineup, the real times from the last race they ran.

It is a projection, not a result. The real races go off Saturday, Nov. 7 at Fortress Obetz.

## How it works

- **The field is the official one.** The teams and individuals are the State qualifiers exactly as Athletic.net posted them from the regional results. Nobody is added, dropped or moved.
- **Every runner carries their regional time.** A runner who ran 16:06 on Saturday runs 16:06 in the mock meet. The four regional courses are different, and the times are not course-adjusted, so a fast course helps and a slow course hurts. Keep that in mind when you read it.
- **Standard cross country scoring.** Five score, sixth and seventh displace, and a team-score tie goes to the team whose sixth runner finished higher. Individual qualifiers run in the finish order but never take a point away from a team runner, the same way they do not at the real State meet.
- **Labeled on every page.** Each division section says "Projected, not official" with the date and time the regional results were pulled.
