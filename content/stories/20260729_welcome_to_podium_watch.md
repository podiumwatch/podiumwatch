Podium Watch now has a dedicated stories section built for Ohio high school running.

## What you will find here

This page will include rankings, athlete spotlights, meet coverage, interviews, and features from around the state.

Every new story will appear at the top of the stories page automatically based on the date listed in the file.

## Built for a weekly cadence

A new story can be added by placing one Markdown file into the `content/stories` folder and deploying the site again. No story card needs to be created by hand.
