# The opening view

Ottawa Hills is the first team on the Girls Division IV board, and its 97 points make it the only team in the classification below 100. Ottawa Hills combines a second place front runner with four more scorers inside the first 29. That is a powerful starting position, but the race behind it is already compressed.

Liberty Center ranks second at 145. Rittman is third at 153. Grandview Heights is fourth at 156. Eleven points cover those three teams, and each lineup reaches its total differently. Liberty Center begins with stronger early placement. Rittman stays controlled through five. Grandview Heights owns the tightest spread of the group. Archbold, Fort Loramie, Minster, and West Liberty-Salem create another close band from fifth through eighth.

Division IV rewards teams that can keep every scorer involved. A front runner can create an advantage, but the fifth position decides whether that advantage survives. The preseason ranking is therefore less about one fast time and more about how seven returning runners interact across a full 25 team field.

# How the rankings were created

These rankings use returning runners and recorded 2025 5K performances. Teams were placed in their official 2026 OHSAA divisions. The twenty five fastest eligible teams by five runner average formed each simulated field, with up to seven returners entered per team.

Five runners scored. Sixth and seventh runners displaced opponents and decided ties. The lowest score won, with ties settled by the sixth runner and then the seventh. The number beside a team is its simulated cross country score, not its total time or five runner average.

The recorded performances came from different courses, meets, weather conditions, and points in the 2025 season. No course adjustment was applied. The result measures returning strength through one consistent scoring model, but it does not guarantee what will happen when teams race in 2026.

# What could change

Summer development, health, race execution, and normal improvement can change this order. One runner moving through a crowded pack can lower a team score while also displacing rival scorers.

The current lineups show several specific opportunities: Liberty Center currently has a 96.06 second spread and a fifth score of 46th, Grandview Hts. currently has a 69.03 second spread and a fifth score of 43rd, and Maplewood currently has a 142.40 second spread and a fifth score of 92nd.
