# The opening view

Mount Gilead enters Division IV and immediately changes the preseason conversation. Russia holds third at 180. Tinora is fourth at 196, Columbus Grove is fifth at 197, and those neighboring scores create another important race. St. Henry, Botkins, Legacy Christian, and Maplewood occupy sixth through ninth within 18 points. Cedarville rounds out the Top 10, but the lineup battles do not stop there.

Russia and Columbus Grove bring runners at the very front. Tinora, St. Henry, and Botkins show what a controlled spread can do. The classification will be shaped by how those different strengths hold up in the same race.

# How the rankings were created

These rankings use returning runners and recorded 2025 5K performances. Teams were placed in their official 2026 OHSAA divisions. The twenty five fastest eligible teams by five runner average formed each simulated field, with up to seven returners entered per team.

Five runners scored. Sixth and seventh runners displaced opponents and decided ties. The lowest score won, with ties settled by the sixth runner and then the seventh. The number beside a team is its simulated cross country score, not its total time or five runner average.

The recorded performances came from different courses, meets, weather conditions, and points in the 2025 season. No course adjustment was applied. The result measures returning strength through one consistent scoring model, but it does not guarantee what will happen when teams race in 2026.
