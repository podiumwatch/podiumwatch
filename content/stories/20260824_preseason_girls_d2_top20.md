# The opening view

The first number that defines Girls Division II is 104. That is Watterson's simulated score, and no other team in the classification is within 100 points. Wadsworth begins second at 240, Avon third at 254, and the gap gives Watterson a significant preseason advantage. It also turns the chase behind the favorite into a compelling race of its own.

Avon brings a remarkably tight five runner spread. Aurora and Granville place important runners near the front. Bay sits one point behind Granville. Canal Winchester follows four points later. From third through seventh, only 18 points separate five teams. Boardman and Tallmadge complete the Top 10, but CVCA is close enough in eleventh to keep the cutoff active.

A large August lead does not make the season automatic. It means Watterson has the best returning baseline under this method. The rest of Division II has a clear target: reduce the late scoring numbers, strengthen the fifth position, and use sixth and seventh runners to take points away from the field.

# How the rankings were created

These rankings use returning runners and recorded 2025 5K performances. Teams were placed in their official 2026 OHSAA divisions. The twenty five fastest eligible teams by five runner average formed each simulated field, with up to seven returners entered per team.

Five runners scored. Sixth and seventh runners displaced opponents and decided ties. The lowest score won, with ties settled by the sixth runner and then the seventh. The number beside a team is its simulated cross country score, not its total time or five runner average.

The recorded performances came from different courses, meets, weather conditions, and points in the 2025 season. No course adjustment was applied. The result measures returning strength through one consistent scoring model, but it does not guarantee what will happen when teams race in 2026.
