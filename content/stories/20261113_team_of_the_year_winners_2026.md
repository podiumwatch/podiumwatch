Voting for the 2026 Podium Watch Year Awards closed Friday at 9 p.m. Readers cast 104,840 votes across 24 categories, and 29,452 of them went to Team of the Year.

Congratulations to the eight teams that won. These awards were decided by reader votes. They are not Podium Watch rankings or official OHSAA honors.

## Boys

**Division I: Medina** (797 votes, 368 ahead of Olentangy Liberty). Medina was 25th in the preseason poll and 16th in the Pre-State poll, then finished eighth at state with 250 points. All five Medina scorers ran personal bests at Fortress Obetz.

**Division II: Hoover** (4,229 votes, 248 ahead of Salem). Hoover held the No. 1 spot in Weeks 2, 3 and 4, entered state ranked 14th and finished third with 217 points.

**Division III: Canal Fulton Northwest** (368 votes, 122 ahead of Wyoming). Northwest was 30th in the preseason poll and 18th in the Pre-State poll, and finished 10th at state with 296 points.

**Division IV: Seneca East** (2,849 votes, 207 ahead of Botkins).

## Girls

**Division I: Westerville North** (1,294 votes, 487 ahead of Hilliard Darby).

**Division II: Fremont Ross** (751 votes, 33 ahead of St. Ursula Academy, the closest Team of the Year race). Fremont Ross was unranked in the preseason poll, rose to fourth in the Pre-State poll and finished fifth at state with 234 points.

**Division III: Huron** (1,751 votes, 128 ahead of Salem). Huron was No. 1 in nine of the season's 11 polls and finished second at state with 125 points, six behind Oakwood.

**Division IV: Dayton Christian** (1,055 votes, 562 ahead of Grandview Heights).
