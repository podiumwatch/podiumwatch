The Ohio Association of Track & Cross Country Coaches and Podium Watch are officially partners.

The partnership, announced today, brings the OATCCC's championship information and Ohio's high school running coverage together on podiumwatch.org. It starts with the 2027 indoor season.

For coaches, it means one place to find what they need from their association. For athletes and families, it means the people who run the sport and the people who cover it are pulling in the same direction.

## Why it makes sense

The OATCCC is the coaches association behind cross country and track and field in Ohio. Its members run the programs, vote in the coaches polls and put on the Indoor State Championships.

Podium Watch started in the spring of 2026 with one goal: give Ohio's runners the recognition they earn. Since then it has grown into a home for results, rankings, team and athlete pages, and stories from every division. On regional meet day this fall, Podium Watch content was viewed more than 400,000 times.

The two already overlapped. Podium Watch has carried the OATCCC coaches polls all season, from the preseason poll through the final one. The partnership makes that connection official and builds on it.

> "Ohio's coaches have built something special in this sport, and they deserve a home that shows it. Podium Watch already covers our athletes every week, so bringing the OATCCC's championships and information under the same roof just made sense. Coaches and families will find what they need faster, and our association can keep its focus where it belongs: on the athletes and the coaches who serve them."
>
> Matt Schock, President, Ohio Association of Track & Cross Country Coaches

## What coaches and families will see

- **Indoor State Meet information in one place.** Key dates, divisions, and eligibility and verification, written to be easy to read on a phone.
- **The official OATCCC coaches polls.** Every division, boys and girls, all season.
- **Coverage of OATCCC events.** From the polls to the championship meets.
- **An OATCCC section on Podium Watch.** The association's information sits under its own name, logo and colors at [podiumwatch.org/oatccc](/oatccc/).
- **Team verification online.** Starting December 1, athletic directors verify their teams for the Indoor State Meet on the site.

> "Ohio has some of the best coaches in the country, and they have been doing this work for a long time. I started Podium Watch to give these kids the recognition they earn. Working with the OATCCC lets us do that with the people who know this sport best."
>
> Zachary Bell, founder, Podium Watch

## What does not change

The OATCCC still sets the rules, deadlines and eligibility. It still runs its championship meets and its polls, and registration stays with its current partners.

Podium Watch stays independent. Its rankings, stories and opinions remain its own, and it stays free for every Ohio runner, coach and parent.

## What's next

The 2027 Indoor State Meet pages are live now at [podiumwatch.org/oatccc](/oatccc/). Verification forms go to athletic directors on December 1, and more OATCCC pages will be added throughout the year.

Questions about the partnership can go to media@podiumwatch.org. Questions about the Indoor State Meet go to the Indoor State Meet Chair, Anjanette Whitman, at awhitman@oatccc.com.

More info at podiumwatch.org.

**About the OATCCC.** The Ohio Association of Track & Cross Country Coaches was founded in 1941. It represents Ohio's cross country and track and field coaches and runs the Indoor State Championships, the coaches polls, the coaches clinics, the Hall of Fame, and scholarship and grant programs.

**About Podium Watch.** Podium Watch is an independent site covering Ohio high school cross country and track and field, with results, rankings, team and athlete pages, and stories from every division.
