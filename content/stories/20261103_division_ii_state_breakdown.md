Division II gave us a two-point regional title and a 35-point team score on the same afternoon. At Tiffin, Lexington beat Bowling Green 74 to 76. At Pickerington, the Watterson girls scored 35. And if you line up every Division II girls regional time, the five fastest all belong to sophomores.

Saturday at Fortress Obetz, the girls run at 11:50 AM and the boys at 12:30 PM. Boardman sent 10 boys teams and nine girls teams, more than any other regional, so the Northeast photos will be all over this race.

Every time below is a real 2026 result. When a team "projects" somewhere, that is the actual 24-team state field scored with each runner's Oct. 31 regional time. Four different courses, not course-adjusted, so it is Projected · not official.

**What to watch Saturday**

- **The most open boys race at Obetz.** Findlay's Russell Stine ran the fastest regional time, 15:18.8, and seven more runners sit between 15:26.5 and 15:33.8.
- **The Shultz-Heithaus rubber match.** Mount Vernon's Calvin Heithaus won their district. Big Walnut's Ethan Shultz won Pickerington by 2.2 seconds.
- **Lexington vs. Bowling Green, again.** Lexington won Tiffin 74-76. On regional times Bowling Green projects 1st at 133 and Lexington 2nd at 141.
- **Sophomores own the girls race.** The five fastest Division II girls regional times all belong to sophomores, led by Celina's Elle Miesse (17:32.0) and Sylvania Southview's Julia Bocik (17:33.3).
- **Watterson's to lose.** Watterson scored 35 at Pickerington and projects 81 points clear of 2nd.
- **A different favorite on season bests.** Turpin's Carson Spencer has the fastest 2026 time in the boys field, 15:08.3. Stine is 4th by that measure.

*Team projections are Projected · not official.*

## Boys Individual Race: Wide Open at the Top

**Stine has the fastest regional time.** Findlay senior Russell Stine won Tiffin in 15:18.8, the best Division II time of the regional round. Sylvania Southview senior Steven Owen was 9.1 seconds back (15:27.9), and St. Francis de Sales (Toledo) junior Brennan Higgins was 3rd in 15:29.8. Stine and Owen both go as individuals.

**Shultz and Heithaus, one apiece.** Big Walnut junior Ethan Shultz won Pickerington in 15:26.5, 2.2 seconds ahead of Mount Vernon sophomore Calvin Heithaus. A week earlier at districts, Heithaus won in 15:13.33 and Shultz was 2nd in 15:18.95. Saturday is the rubber match.

**Troy.** Turpin junior Carson Spencer won in 15:31.0. Tippecanoe senior Everett Muhlenkamp was 2nd (15:42.1) and Loveland senior Ian Trombley 3rd (15:44.9), with Anderson's Elijah Trickey (15:49.3) and Turpin senior Caleb Anderson (15:55.4) next.

## Boys Team Race: Two Points at Tiffin, Again on Saturday?

**Lexington 74, Bowling Green 76.** The Northwest Regional title came down to two points. Lexington won it behind senior Will Hooper (4th, 15:37.5), junior Christian Estep (5th, 15:40.6), senior John Bartone (10th, 15:50.5), sophomore Mathias Bender (16th, 16:12.8) and junior Chidalu Ezike (42nd, 17:00.7).

Bowling Green answered with four in the top 14: senior Sam Wiles (15:41.0), junior Lloyd Waldron (15:48.9), sophomore Rece Simon (16:00.9) and junior Adam Proulx (16:07.3). Score the state field on regional times and the order flips. Bowling Green projects 1st at 133 and Lexington 2nd at 141 (Projected · not official).

**Big Walnut has the top runner.** Big Walnut won Pickerington with 97 behind Shultz, senior Frank Evener (18th, 16:14.1), sophomore Jalen Jones (20th, 16:16.1), junior Jason Shady (25th, 16:24.5) and senior Samuel Cochran (41st, 16:52.9). It projects 3rd at 173. St. Francis de Sales (Toledo), 3rd at Tiffin with 111 behind Higgins and senior Spencer Frangou (7th, 15:45.5), projects 4th.

**The Troy teams.** Loveland won Troy with 80 and projects 5th at 245. Trombley led, then junior Keene Wolfer (16th, 16:34.2), sophomore Quinn Mentzer (19th, 16:36.3), sophomore Benjamin Slater (21st, 16:41.8) and freshman Trey Strole (25th, 16:49.6). Turpin was 2nd with 91 behind Spencer and Anderson, two of the top five.

## Girls Team Race: Watterson's to Lose

**Thirty-five points.** Watterson won Pickerington with 35, the lowest girls score at any Division II regional. Van Zandt won, sophomore Karine Bhowmick was 5th (18:24.2), junior Campbell Retzke 6th (18:28.1), junior Berkley LaCount 8th (18:55.0) and freshman Bernadette Dziewiatkowski 17th (19:33.5). On regional times Watterson projects 1st at 75, a full 81 points clear of 2nd (Projected · not official).

**Fremont Ross and Aurora chase.** Fremont Ross won Tiffin with 66 behind three underclassmen in the top seven: Kiser, sophomore Julia Norris (5th, 18:37.7) and freshman Brooklynn Eisenhauer (7th, 18:46.2). It projects 2nd at 156. Aurora won Boardman with 111 behind three sophomores in the top 11, Miller, Claire Jenkins (18:47.5) and Evelyn Jenkins (18:53.2), and projects 3rd at 177.

Pickerington's depth shows up next. Granville (87), Big Walnut (85) and Canal Winchester (96) project 4th, 5th and 6th. Big Walnut ran the tightest pack of the three, 48.6 seconds from one to five.

**Troy.** Bellbrook won with 94 behind seniors Morgan Dalton (5th, 19:23.4) and Allison High (7th, 19:27.3), with its five inside 58.5 seconds. Turpin was 2nd with 108 behind Lewis, and St. Ursula Academy was 3rd with 123 behind sophomore Caroline Josshua (4th, 19:10.3).

## Around the Regionals: Tiffin and Pickerington

**Tiffin, boys.** Findlay senior Russell Stine won in 15:18.8, 9.1 seconds clear of Sylvania Southview senior Steven Owen. Lexington edged Bowling Green 74 to 76, and St. Francis de Sales (Toledo) and Sylvania Northview took the other two spots. Lima Shawnee missed by seven points. Stine has company from Findlay, because junior Camden Stechschulte also qualified as an individual (11th, 15:54.0).

**Tiffin, girls.** Celina sophomore Elle Miesse beat Sylvania Southview sophomore Julia Bocik by 1.3 seconds, and Fremont Ross sophomore Lily Kiser was 3rd. Fremont Ross won with 66. Lexington (111), Anthony Wayne (113) and Bowling Green (114) took the last three spots, three points apart. Celina missed as a team, but Miesse and junior Sophie Gudorf (12th, 19:08.6) both qualified.

**Pickerington, boys.** Big Walnut junior Ethan Shultz beat Mount Vernon sophomore Calvin Heithaus by 2.2 seconds, and Columbus DeSales senior Lucas Steele was 3rd in 15:36.3. Big Walnut won with 97. Watterson (147), Unioto (149), Granville (155) and Columbus DeSales (169) also qualified, so Watterson sends both its boys and girls to Obetz. Dublin Scioto missed by four points.

**Pickerington, girls.** Watterson junior Kaitlyn Van Zandt won in 17:57.0, 13.4 seconds ahead of Granville junior Elsa Tonnessen, and Watterson put all five scorers in the top 17 for 35 points. Big Walnut (85), Granville (87), Canal Winchester (96) and Columbus DeSales (194) also qualified. Athens junior Zoe Wayner was 4th in 18:15.9 and goes as an individual.
