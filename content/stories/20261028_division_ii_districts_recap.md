Calvin Heithaus is a sophomore. On Saturday, he ran faster than every other Division II boy in Ohio.

The Mount Vernon runner won his race at Hilliard Darby in 15:13.3. His team qualified for regionals too, so he goes to Pickerington with his teammates.

He'll have company up front. Seven Division II boys broke 15:24 on Saturday:

On the girls side, the teams we picked in August delivered. Watterson, our preseason No. 1, won its district. So did three of the teams we named as its closest chasers: Wadsworth, Aurora and Granville.

*At Cedarville University and Voice of America Park, the top two teams share the district title.*

## Central

Hilliard Darby district · Pickerington regional

**Watterson's wave.** Junior Kaitlyn Van Zandt won Girls Race 2, and four teammates followed her into the top eight. Watterson scored 22.

**Hughey keeps getting faster.** Canal Winchester senior Peyton Hughey won Girls Race 1 in 17:46.3, almost 50 seconds faster than her winning time at Piqua in September. Granville won the team title.

**Steele by a step.** Columbus DeSales senior Lucas Steele won Boys Race 1 by less than two seconds over Granville's Logan Miller. Granville won the team title there too.

**Big Walnut's depth.** Big Walnut put seven runners in the top 19 of Boys Race 2 and won the title ahead of Watterson.

## East

Cambridge district · Pickerington regional

Only three Division II boys teams advance from the East, and Dover won the title. New Philadelphia senior Kevin Smith won the race.

John Glenn senior Ashlynn Kuklica won the girls race by 39 seconds, and her team took the last regional spot. New Philadelphia won the team title.

## Northeast

GlenOak, Lorain County CC and Madison districts · Boardman regional

**Tight at GlenOak.** Wadsworth won the girls title, but the top four teams finished within 19 points of each other. Boardman senior Olivia Brady won the race. Hoover won the boys title out of 24 teams, the biggest Division II boys field in the state.

**CVCA, one-two-three.** Isaac Solomon, Zac Andamasaris and Andrew Clark swept the top three places at Lorain County Community College, and CVCA, our preseason No. 1, won the boys title. The CVCA girls won too.

**Audrey Miller.** Aurora's sophomore ran 17:17.6 at Madison, nearly a minute ahead of second place. Teammates Claire and Evelyn Jenkins were second and third. Nordonia junior Anderson Seredick won the boys race, and Kenston won the team title.

## Northwest

Leaders Farms and Owens CC districts · Tiffin regional

**Bocik by a mile.** Sylvania Southview sophomore Julia Bocik won at Leaders Farms by 1 minute, 37.8 seconds. Bowling Green won the girls title, and St. Francis de Sales (Toledo) edged Bowling Green for the boys title.

**Stine and Lexington.** Findlay senior Russell Stine won at Owens Community College, and Lexington, second in our preseason poll, won the team title by 84 points. Celina sophomore Elle Miesse won the girls race.

## Southeast

Rio Grande district · Pickerington regional

**Eighteen-hundredths.** Gallia Academy senior Lane Shaffer won the boys race by less than a fifth of a second over Chillicothe's Levi Wood. Unioto won the team title, and Logan won the girls title.

## Southwest

Cedarville University and Voice of America districts · Troy regional

**Bellbrook, twice.** Bellbrook won both Division II titles at Cedarville, sharing the girls title with Butler and the boys title with Carroll.

**Turpin's day.** At Voice of America, Turpin junior Carson Spencer won the boys race and sophomore Colby Lewis won the girls race. Turpin shares both team titles.

## Up next

Regionals are Saturday, Oct. 31. The Division II state races at Fortress Obetz are Saturday, Nov. 7: girls at 11:50 a.m., boys at 12:30 p.m.
