In consideration for participating with the OAT&CCC Indoor Middle School State Championship Meet all of the following regulations must be complied with:

  • The participating Indoor Track & Field team’s school must be a member of the OHSAA for the 2025-2026 academic year and abides by the regulations to be member in good-standing with the OHSAA;
  • All coaches representing the participating Indoor Track & Field team must be 2026 members of the OAT&CCC;
  • All student-athletes associated with the participating Indoor Track & Field team must meet all local Board of Education and OHSAA eligibility requirements for participation in athletics; and
  • If the Indoor Track & Field team participating is represented by a coach or student-athlete who does not meet the above regulations, then the OATCCC may exclude the team or any of its competitors or coaches from the Indoor State Meet and apply sanctions to the team's future participation with OATCCC events.
