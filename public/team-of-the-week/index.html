How Team of the Week Works

1

Nominate

Submit an Ohio high school boys or girls team that delivered an outstanding performance.

2

Finalists

Podium Watch reviews the nominations and selects separate boys and girls finalists.

3

Vote

The public votes for the boys and girls teams they believe deserve the weekly honor.

4

Winners

One boys team and one girls team are announced and added to the Podium Watch archive.
